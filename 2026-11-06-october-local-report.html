Your 40 tracked locations ended October in the Local Pack for 71 of the 270 keywords tracked since the 3 October baseline, up from 55 (+16, or +29%). Map top-3 placements on the same keywords rose from 58 to 84 (+26). Across all 277 keywords now tracked, locations appear in the Local Pack for 73 (26.4%) and rank in the map top 3 for 87 (31.4%).

The direction is positive, but this is one month of data and the weekly line is noisy. Most of the gain sits in smaller-market branches, Arcadia and the Illinois benchmark group. The 16 Illinois page-build branches held steady in the Local Pack (36 to 35) while gaining map top-3 placements (35 to 43). That is expected at this stage: the first service-area page (Peoria) went live on 26 October, the same day as the last October run, so none of October's movement can be credited to the pages yet.

Source: BrightLocal Rank Tracker, runs of 3, 12, 19 and 26 October 2026, Google US, search depth 50. Changes compare the same keyword at the same location on both dates.

Reading this data

Read the start-to-finish change, not any single week.

  • Google anti-scraping. Since mid-September Google has served some rank trackers altered results pages. BrightLocal reports Rank Tracker is affected, with Local Pack and map results hit less than organic, and positions 10 and below least reliable. This likely explains the 12 October high and the 19 October dip.
  • Baseline timing. The 3 October baseline ran during Google's September spam update, so part of October's rise may be recovery rather than new gains.
  • Local search grid not re-run. The latest grid scans are from 2 October. The November report will include a fresh grid run as a second, unaffected check.
  • Keyword sets differ by location. Locations track 3 to 11 keywords each, so compare locations on share and change rather than raw counts.
  • A separate pilot metric. The proposal's target (10.4% of 231 location and keyword pairs on page 1 of Google Places, rising to 28% to 40%) is a different test. It will be re-run on 17 November and reported on its own, not mixed with these figures.
  • Not yet in this report. The 2 November run, review counts and ratings since the baseline, and the review pilot's first-month numbers.

November recommendations

The fastest wins in November are listing fixes Addus can make in Yext and pointing the Peoria profile at its new page.

Listings (Addus in Yext, DRA to confirm on the live profiles)

  • Point the East Peoria Google profile to the new Peoria page instead of the addus.com homepage. This is the pilot's first page and listing pairing to measure.
  • Give Barrington home health its own phone number; it currently shares the hospice line.
  • Change Palos Heights home health's website to journeycare.org and its Facebook link to JourneyCare's page.
  • Set the primary category to Hospice on Barrington and Deerfield hospice, and remove "Corporate office" from Palos Heights hospice.
  • Relink Facebook in Yext. One relink may restore all three pilot pages and up to 42 others across the account.
  • Approve the profile description and review response templates sent 6 October so they can roll out.

Reviews

  • Edit the six older owner responses on Barrington and Palos Heights hospice that confirm a family member's care (DRA).
  • Share the review pilot's first-month numbers so the November report can show reviews beside rankings (Addus).

Pages

  • Answer the Niles and Quincy questions from 30 October so both pages can be finalized (Addus).
  • Send Crystal Lake hospice feedback so the first JourneyCare page can be built (Addus).

Reporting

  • Run a fresh local search grid scan before the November comparison (DRA).
  • Add review count and rating change per location to the monthly view (DRA).

The Chicago metro needs sustained effort beyond November. Chicago (95th St) has 91 reviews at 2.3 stars with 58 negative reviews unanswered as of 29 September, and it does not reach the Local Pack for any tracked keyword.
