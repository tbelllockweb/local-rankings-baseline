        Reading this data

        What each figure measures, where it comes from, and the gaps to close before the first monthly comparison.

        Local Pack and map rank

        From BrightLocal Rank Tracker, run 3 October 2026 at a search depth of 50. "In the Local Pack" means the listing appeared in Google's three-business map pack for that keyword. Map rank is the listing's position in the Local Finder (the full map list).

        BrightLocal · Rank Tracker · Google US

        Local Search Grid

        The latest grid scan per keyword, run 2 October 2026. Each scan checks rank at 25 or 49 points around the location. Grid average is the mean rank across points; points outside the top 20 count against the average. "Grid points in the top 3" is the share of all points where the listing ranks 1 to 3.

        BrightLocal · Local Search Grid

        Averages

        Average map rank only counts keywords where the listing ranks in the top 50, so read it alongside the count of keywords not ranking. Client and portfolio figures pool every keyword rather than averaging location averages.

        Citations excluded this week

        Citation Tracker scans from 2 October returned no citations for any location. A re-run on Quincy found 10 of 38 key citations (score 28/100) and flagged a wrong suite number on Data Axle and YellowPages. Citations rejoin the baseline after the 9 October scheduled run.

        Keyword sets differ by location

        Locations track between 3 and 11 Rank Tracker keywords and 2 to 5 grid keywords, with different mixes of city and "near me" terms. Compare locations on coverage and averages rather than raw counts, or standardize the sets before the first monthly comparison.
